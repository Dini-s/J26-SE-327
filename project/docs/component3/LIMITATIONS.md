# Current Limitations

- Spring PetClinic is the primary integration
  corpus and does not provide complete industrial
  SRS documentation.

- Some requirements and evidence items are
  controlled research fixtures and are explicitly
  labelled.

- Controlled performance evidence must not be
  represented as real production JMeter or k6
  execution evidence.

- Automated adequacy is strongest for functional
  evidence and measurable performance evidence.

- Reliability, scalability, security,
  accessibility, maintainability, availability
  and usability currently require specialized
  evidence or human review.

- RVES is experimental until sensitivity,
  ablation and expert-rating evaluation is
  completed.

- Controlled revalidation scenarios exercise the
  intended change-context contract before live
  Component 4 integration.

- Semantic retrieval discovers candidate
  evidence. It is not proof of requirement
  validation.

- Two PostgresIntegrationTests source methods in
  the current PetClinic corpus do not have a
  matching Surefire execution result and remain
  explicitly unresolved.
