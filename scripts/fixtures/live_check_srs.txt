Online Banking - Sample Requirements

1. Functional requirements

1.1 The system shall allow a registered customer to transfer money between their own accounts.
1.2 The system shall send an email receipt to the customer within 60 seconds after a transfer is completed.

2. Non-functional requirements

2.1 The system shall respond to 95% of balance enquiries within 2 seconds under a load of 500 concurrent users.
2.2 The system should process payments quickly.
2.3 The user interface shall be user-friendly.

3. Security

3.1 The system shall lock a customer account after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
